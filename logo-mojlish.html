Mojlish

An identity for our band, built on friendship and made to grow with whatever we create next.

About the project

Our band.
Our shared identity.

Mojlish is the band formed by my friends and me: Dip, Akib, Mahir, Niaz, Wasif and Arnob. It is a shared creative space for our group, with friendship at its heart.

I made our first logo years ago, but over time I felt it lacked a distinct identity. I returned to the project to build a visual language we could call our own, giving the band a consistent foundation for more work in the future.

The redesign arranges four illustrated panels around a central Bengali wordmark. A poetry book, an artist’s tools, a hammer and sickle, and a flute give each part of the composition a distinct visual role while the central lettering ties them together.

The presentation develops the identity through individual symbol studies, an outline stage and a final colour treatment. Warm cream and pink sit alongside darker accents, while the expressive Bengali wordmark uses Li Mastarmoshai Unicode. T-shirt and mug mockups show the resulting composition in use.

  • Bengali wordmark and colour development
  • T-shirt and mug applications
