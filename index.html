01 / About

Hello, I'm Niaz. I am a Creative Visual Designer currently working at Tahams and a Data Science & Analytics student at East West University in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

I create expressive Bengali typography, clean brand designs, vector illustrations, and event posters. By blending modern design with local language and culture, I craft visual work that truly connects with people.
